AWS offers the broadest selection of mature, fully featured cloud services globally.
2. **Which provider best integrates with Microsoft technologies?**
Microsoft Azure seamlessly integrates with Windows Server, Active Directory, and Microsoft 365.
3. **Which provider is strongest in Artificial Intelligence and Kubernetes?**
GCP leads the industry in Kubernetes management via GKE and advanced AI tools like Vertex AI and BigQuery.
4. **Which cloud platform would you personally choose and why?**
I would choose AWS due to its massive market adoption, extensive documentation, and overall reliability for hosting general infrastructure.
